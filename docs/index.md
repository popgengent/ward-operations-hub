# Ward Operations & Collaboration Hub

!!! warning "Scope & Policy Notice"
    **This site does not establish Church policy.** Everything documented here reflects local ward traditions, historical lessons learned, personal preferences, and practical collaboration tips. Always defer to the *General Handbook* and current priesthood leadership keys.

## Purpose of This Hub
When callings change, valuable institutional knowledge is often lost. This site's purpose is to preserve *some* of that knowledge by documenting current administrative practices to improve coordination across organizations, document past experiences, and help members get up to speed quickly in new callings. In summary, the purpose of this site is to suggest a *general* baseline of administrative practices in order to enable members to participate in deeper and more personal ministering. In particular, this site focuses on cross-organization collaboration. Please do not use this site to replace the *General Handbook*, or allow the suggestions and past experiences outlined here to interfere with *personal direction* that you have received. 

This site is meant to serve as a living document and is meant to be updated. If your usual practices conflict with information shown here, [updating the information]( https://popgengent.github.io/ward-operations-hub/governance/) will increase collaborative capacity and create a memory of past administrative practices that may be useful to the next ward council.
